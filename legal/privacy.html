Privacy Policy

What TRADE-AI collects, why, who processes it and the choices you have.

Last updated 15 November 2026

The short version: we collect as little as we can. There's no advertising and no tracking cookies, and we never sell your data. Without an account we keep no personal data at all, only anonymous visit counts.

1. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address and a scrambled ("hashed") copy of your passwordWhen you create an accountTo sign you in, reset your password and send emails you ask for
Signal unlocks and trial usageWhen you unlock a signalTo run the 3-signal free trial
Subscription status, renewal dates and a link to your billing portalWhen you subscribeTo give paid access. Card details go to Lemon Squeezy only, and we never see them.
Alert preferences and a browser push addressWhen you turn on alertsTo send the notifications or emails you asked for
Anonymous visit countsWhen you open a pageTo see which pages are used. No cookies are involved, and IP addresses are never stored (see below).
Basic server logsOn each requestSecurity and fixing errors. Kept briefly.

We don't collect exchange API keys, wallet keys, identity documents or card numbers.

2. Visit statistics

We count visits without cookies. We combine your IP address and browser name with a secret that changes every day, scramble the result into a code that can't be reversed (a "hash") and keep only that code. It can't be turned back into your IP address or linked across days, and it's deleted after 2 days. If your browser sends a Do-Not-Track or Global Privacy Control signal, you aren't counted at all.

3. Browser storage

The app stores your sign-in session and display preferences in your browser's local storage. These are not tracking cookies, and signing out removes the session.

4. Who processes your data

  • Neon: database hosting (EU).
  • Ample: application server hosting (USA).
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • Brevo: email delivery.
  • Lemon Squeezy: payments, tax and invoices.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google or Apple): notification delivery.
  • Market-data providers: your browser fetches prices directly from Binance and Hyperliquid, so they see your IP address under their own privacy policies.

We share data only so these services can work, never for advertising, and we never sell it.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside Sri Lanka, including in the EU and the USA, under their own safeguards.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Password-reset links: expire after 30 minutes.
  • Daily visit codes: deleted after 2 days. Only aggregate counts are kept.
  • Billing records: kept by Lemon Squeezy as tax law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Account page → Delete account. This removes your account and unlock history.
  • Notifications and emails: turn them off on the Alerts page, or with the link in any alert email.
  • Access, correction or other requests: contact us. Depending on where you live, laws such as Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act or the GDPR may give you further rights.

8. Security

Passwords are stored only in hashed form (scrypt). Connections use HTTPS. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and accounts lock temporarily after repeated failures. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

9. Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top. For significant changes, we'll show a notice in the app or email you.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through our GitHub issues page.
