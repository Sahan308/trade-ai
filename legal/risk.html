Risk disclaimer

Crypto trading is high risk. TRADE-AI is an analysis tool, not financial advice, and never trades for you.

Last updated 15 November 2026

The short version: you can lose some or all of your money trading crypto, and much faster with leverage. Nothing on TRADE-AI tells you what to do with your money. Every decision, and its outcome, is yours.

1. Not financial advice

TRADE-AI provides market data, technical indicators, chart-pattern and Smart Money Concepts readings, a market scanner, futures data and risk calculators for information and education only. Nothing on the website, in the app, in notifications or in emails is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset. TRADE-AI is not a licensed financial adviser, broker or exchange.

2. TRADE-AI never trades for you

TRADE-AI does not connect to your exchange account, does not ask for exchange API keys, does not hold your funds and never places orders. You place every trade yourself, on a platform of your choice.

3. Signals are experimental

Trade signals are produced by an experimental rule-based strategy. In our published backtests (see the research results), the strategy lost money after fees, and none of the variants we tested passed our pre-registered criteria. Signals are shown for research and transparency. Do not treat them as recommendations.

4. Scores are not probabilities

A signal's "setup score" counts how many of the strategy's conditions lined up. It is not the chance that a trade will win, and a higher score does not mean a trade is more likely to be profitable.

5. Past results don't predict the future

Backtests, historical signal outcomes and examples are hypothetical or past results. They include assumptions about fills, fees and slippage that may differ from real trading. Markets change, and past performance does not indicate future results.

6. Leverage and futures

Futures and margin trading magnify both gains and losses. A small price move can cause liquidation and the loss of your whole position. Funding payments, fees and slippage add to your costs. The position-size and liquidation calculators are estimates and can differ from your exchange's own calculations.

7. Data can be wrong or late

Market data comes from third parties such as Binance and Hyperliquid and may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable in your region or wrong. Analysis is based on closed candles and can lag live prices. Always check prices on your exchange before acting.

8. Your responsibility

Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Make sure crypto trading is legal for you where you live, and consider speaking to a licensed financial adviser. You are solely responsible for your trading decisions and their results.
